# Mobile deployment: ownership affects management decisions

Separate device ownership, allowed use, and management authority.

| Model | Expanded term | Study distinction |
|---|---|---|
| BYOD | Bring Your Own Device | Personally owned device used for work |
| COPE | Corporate-Owned, Personally Enabled | Organization-owned device with permitted personal use |
| CYOD | Choose Your Own Device | User chooses from an approved selection; verify the ownership terms |

Mobile device management (MDM) can enforce supported settings and lifecycle actions. Its capabilities depend on platform, enrollment, and policy. Do not assume every enrollment grants full access to personal content or permits a full-device wipe.

Worked example: a worker leaves and their personal phone contains managed business data. Follow the approved offboarding process and verify whether selective removal of managed data is supported and authorized. A full wipe is not an automatic default.

Document recovery, separation of work data, privacy expectations, updates, and lost-device response before deployment.

Check yourself: identify what must be checked before remotely wiping a device. Changing ownership model does not remove identity or data-protection requirements.

1. Leave the 46 Cisco-tagged notes outside this Security+ review; no deck reorganization has been performed.
2. Review the one non-Basic note before any bulk field conversion. Its fields differ from Front and Back.
3. Review the repeated-front candidate without deleting either note automatically.
4. Check that image-dependent questions remain understandable when exported with their media.
5. Review reasoning cues that directly reveal the answer. A cue should help recall the concept without naming the choice.
6. Check transcription artifacts such as Security1 appearing in place of Security+.
7. Validate answers against the relevant source. Structural checks do not confirm answer accuracy.

These are review tasks. The uploaded deck has not been modified, and a full answer-by-answer fact check has not been completed.

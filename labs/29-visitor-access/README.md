# Exercise 29: review a visitor's access

[Control selection](../../study-guides/security-controls.md) · [Contents](../../study/contents.md)

Offline fictional exercise. Partial SY0-701 1.2 physical security. Ready to try.

## The policy

Visitors require confirmed sponsorship, a temporary badge, and an escort in the equipment room. The escort must remain present. A badge does not grant unescorted access. Deliveries use a designated receiving area.

| Event | Observation |
|---|---|
| A | A sponsored, badged visitor enters with the assigned escort |
| B | A badged visitor waits outside and asks another employee to hold the door |
| C | An escort leaves a visitor alone in the equipment room |
| D | A courier is redirected to the receiving area |

Decide which events meet the policy. State the next action for each mismatch without inventing intent.

<details>
<summary>Worked review</summary>

A and D meet the stated conditions. For B, verify the required sponsorship and escort through the established process before admitting the visitor. A badge alone does not satisfy the rule. For C, restore required supervision and report/document the lapse through the local process.

The sample does not establish that the visitor is malicious. Access rules can be enforced without assigning a motive.

</details>

## Distinguish control roles

A locked entrance restricts entry. A camera can provide observation and evidence when recording and review are in place. The camera does not itself stop the door from opening. An escort implements a procedural requirement.

For a game connection, sketch the movement of a visitor around a fictional facility. Label policy checks on the drawing. Do not assume a game implements visitor identity or physical security controls.

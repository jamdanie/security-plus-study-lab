# Exercise 17: review a supplier proposal

[Read first](../../study-guides/third-party-risk.md) · [Contents](../../study/contents.md)

Offline fictional exercise. Partial SY0-701 5.3. Status: ready to try.

## The requirement

A team needs a records service. Its stated requirements are recovery within eight hours, a usable data export at exit, and identified incident notification responsibilities.

The proposal includes:
- A signed confidentiality agreement.
- An availability target of 99.9%.
- A demonstration of importing records.
- No recovery test evidence, export demonstration, or notification responsibilities.

## Your task

Write a review with one row for each requirement. State what evidence is missing and the next action. Explain whether the confidentiality agreement resolves any of the three missing items.

Then describe what needs review if a new subcontractor receives access to the records after purchase.

<details>
<summary>Worked review</summary>

| Requirement | Gap | Next action |
|---|---|---|
| Eight-hour recovery | Availability target does not establish recovery time | Request an explicit recovery commitment, responsibilities, and relevant test evidence |
| Usable exit export | Import demo does not demonstrate export | Request an export demonstration and evaluate its format |
| Notification responsibilities | No responsible parties or process identified | Resolve who reports what, to whom, and within the agreed terms |

The confidentiality agreement addresses a different need. Its presence does not demonstrate any of these three requirements.

For the subcontractor change, review access, data flow, applicable requirements, supporting evidence, and approval. Document unresolved risks and route the decision to the responsible owner.

</details>

## Save your result

Keep your review and explain one case where the proposal answers a different question from the requirement. Do not record the supplier as approved while the required evidence is unresolved.

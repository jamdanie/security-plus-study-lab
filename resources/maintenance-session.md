## Pair it with a visual tool

In a game or simulator you already own, choose a supported maintenance task and draw the affected equipment and dependencies. Keep its actual observations separate from the supplied vulnerability and access records.

A repair animation does not establish vulnerability remediation. A worker disappearing from a game does not establish that real accounts, badges, and tokens were revoked.

## Change one condition

Remove the internet exposure, change a finding's applicability, or make an application identity independent of the main login. Explain how the new evidence changes your next action.

These original tasks support selected SY0-701 4.3 and 4.6 concepts. They do not establish a real organization's compliance or a learner's complete exam readiness.

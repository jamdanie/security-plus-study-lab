# Choose a hands-on mission

[Tool directory](visual-and-gamified-learning.md) · [Contents](../study/contents.md)

Choose the question that is giving you trouble. Finish one activity and explain the result before moving to the next. Durations below are suggested study budgets, not measured completion times.

| Question | Mission | Budget | What to keep |
|---|---|---|---|
| Is encoded text protected? | [CyberChef comparison](../labs/20-cyberchef-basics/README.md) | 20 minutes | Input, output, and why decoding needs no secret |
| What does a packet filter change? | [Wireshark saved capture](../labs/21-wireshark-display/README.md) | 25 minutes | Filename, filter, displayed result, and limit |
| Where does the traffic go? | [Packet Tracer observation](../labs/18-packet-tracer-visual/README.md) | 25 minutes | Predicted and observed path |
| Do two paths remove every failure? | [Resilience drawing](../labs/19-visual-resilience/README.md) | 15 minutes | Shared dependency and proposed change |
| Which inventory record needs correction? | [Rack reconciliation](../labs/22-rack-inventory/README.md) | 20 minutes | Evidence and next verification |
| Is a green link light enough? | [Change rehearsal](../labs/23-change-rehearsal/README.md) | 15 minutes | Functional checks and rollback criterion |

## Match the format to your setup

No installation: use the offline drawing, CSV, or change review. A browser: try the supplied CyberChef text. Installed tools: use Packet Tracer or a saved capture in Wireshark.

Already own a data center game? Use [these reflection prompts](steam-data-center-games.md) with supported actions. Buying a game is not needed for the offline missions.

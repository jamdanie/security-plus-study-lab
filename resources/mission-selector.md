## Decide what to revisit

A correct guess still needs review. Explain which condition decided the answer and why the nearest alternative fails. Then change one fact and decide again.

Keep your actual result in a [learning note](../study/hands-on-notes.md). The selector provides activities, not automatic completion tracking or a predicted exam score.

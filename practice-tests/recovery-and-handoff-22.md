# Recovery and handoff 22

Four original questions. Partial SY0-701 3.4/5.2 (1–2) and 4.8/4.9 (3–4). This targeted set is not a weighted mock exam.

[Session](../resources/recovery-session.md) · [Contents](../study/contents.md)

## 1. Orders needs identity restored first. Orders is the highest-priority business service. Which task must precede its restore?

- A. Reports because it is shorter.
- B. Identity because it is a prerequisite.
- C. Any task selected at random.
- D. None; business priority removes dependencies.

## 2. One team restores identity for 20 minutes, then orders for 30. Durations include validation. When is orders available?

- A. 20 minutes.
- B. 30 minutes.
- C. 50 minutes.
- D. 65 minutes.

## 3. An external check fails at 10:00 and passes at 10:08. What is established?

- A. Eight minutes separate these observations.
- B. The outage lasted exactly eight minutes.
- C. A router definitely failed.
- D. Every user recovered at 10:08.

## 4. Two users still report errors after one passed external check. What belongs in the handoff?

- A. Everything is fixed.
- B. The users must be wrong.
- C. The cause is definitely malicious.
- D. The reports remain unresolved and require investigation.

<details>
<summary>Answers and explanations</summary>

1. **B.** B applies the stated dependency. A optimizes the wrong service. C ignores evidence. D confuses business priority with technical capability.

2. **C.** C adds the sequential durations. A counts identity alone. B counts orders alone. D includes the later reports task.

3. **A.** A stays within the records. B assumes exact boundaries. C invents a component cause. D generalizes one check to all users.

4. **D.** D preserves the unresolved evidence. A ignores it. B dismisses it without checking. C invents intent.

</details>

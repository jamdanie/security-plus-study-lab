# Governance practice set 6

Four original single-answer questions, partially aligned to SY0-701 5.1. Use the document purposes and authority boundaries stated in the guide. These are not actual exam questions or a full mock exam.

## Question 1

Technicians need the required sequence for disabling accounts after an approved departure. Which document best fits?

A. Policy
B. Procedure
C. Guideline
D. Risk register

## Question 2

A document mandates a specific approved configuration for remote administration. Under the guide's document hierarchy, what is it?

A. Standard
B. Optional guideline
C. Incident timeline
D. Vendor questionnaire

## Question 3

A document says sensitive records must be limited to authorized users across the organization. What purpose does that statement most directly serve?

A. Document a completed restore test
B. List every help-desk command
C. Establish policy direction
D. Record a scan false positive

## Question 4

An administrator can change permissions, but a request would alter approved business access requirements. What is the best next step?

A. Assume technical ability grants all approval authority
B. Treat the request as an optional suggestion
C. Change the policy silently to match the request
D. Obtain the assigned authority's decision through the approved process

## Explained answers

<details>
<summary>Open after answering</summary>

### 1: B

A procedure gives the sequence. A policy establishes expectations; a guideline recommends approaches; a risk register tracks risks rather than the operational steps.

### 2: A

A standard supplies mandatory detail. B is optional advice; C records incident events; D gathers vendor information.

### 3: C

C sets the organizational expectation. A and D record specific results; B describes implementation detail rather than the broad rule.

### 4: D

D distinguishes authority from capability. A assumes authority, B ignores the requirement change, and C bypasses review and approval.

</details>

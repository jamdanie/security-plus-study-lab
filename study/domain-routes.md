## 1. General security concepts

Read [control selection](../study-guides/security-controls.md), [cryptography choices](../study-guides/cryptography-choices.md), and [zero trust](../study-guides/zero-trust.md).

Try [cryptographic control selection](../labs/06-choose-cryptographic-controls/README.md) and [access decisions](../labs/16-zero-trust-decisions/README.md). Explain what the selected control achieves and which condition permits access.

Review [Controls 7](../practice-tests/controls-07.md) and questions 1–3 in [Trust and suppliers 14](../practice-tests/trust-and-suppliers-14.md).

## 2. Threats and mitigations

Read [phishing](../study-guides/phishing.md), [attack evidence](../study-guides/attack-patterns.md), and [web attack distinctions](../concepts/web-attack-distinctions.md).

Try [message triage](../labs/09-message-triage/README.md). Separate what the evidence shows from what you suspect.

Review [Attack patterns 8](../practice-tests/attack-patterns-08.md). Return to the explanation when you recognize a label but cannot explain the mechanism.

## 3. Security architecture

Read [cloud responsibility](../study-guides/cloud-responsibility.md), [data states](../concepts/data-states.md), and [backup and recovery](../study-guides/backup-and-recovery.md).

Try [recovery objectives](../labs/08-recovery-objectives/README.md). Calculate data loss and service restoration separately.

Review [Cloud 5](../practice-tests/cloud-05.md) and the recovery examples. These topics do not cover every architecture choice.

## 4. Security operations

Read [identity and access](../study-guides/identity-and-access.md), [vulnerability management](../study-guides/vulnerability-management.md), and [incident response](../study-guides/incident-response.md).

Try [access review](../labs/02-access-review/README.md) and [sign-in evidence](../labs/05-sign-in-log-review/README.md). Explain the next action and what evidence would verify it.

Review [Foundations 2](../practice-tests/foundations-02.md). Use the [contents](contents.md#4-security-operations) for wireless, baselines, assets, and other operations topics.

## 5. Program management and oversight

Read [governance documents](../study-guides/security-governance.md), [risk management](../study-guides/risk-management.md), and [third-party risk](../study-guides/third-party-risk.md).

Try [supplier review](../labs/17-supplier-review/README.md). Match requested evidence to the requirement.

Review [Governance 6](../practice-tests/governance-06.md) and questions 4–6 in [Trust and suppliers 14](../practice-tests/trust-and-suppliers-14.md).

## Add a visual practice session

For network basics, try [watching packet events](../labs/18-packet-tracer-visual/README.md) before reviewing segmentation. For availability, trace the [resilience drawing](../labs/19-visual-resilience/README.md) and identify the shared dependency. Use the [resource directory](../resources/visual-and-gamified-learning.md) for beginner challenges or optional paid games.

Keep a [hands-on note](hands-on-notes.md) that separates your prediction, observed result, and explanation. These activities support selected concepts; they do not replace the full domain objectives.

## When to move on

Before leaving a topic, explain a decision without the answer choices. Record the missed distinction, revisit it after a break, and try a changed example. Use [Anki](../anki/README.md) for available cards and the [review plan](review-plan.md) to structure the next session.
